# Call → Follow-up

**Supporting project · working offline prototype**

## Problem
Notes often get lost between a sales call and the next message.

## Try it
Open [the demo](../index.html) and choose Call → Follow-up in the sidebar.

Enter the buyer’s name, stated goal and agreed next step → generate and edit the follow-up.

## Example
Goal: improve qualification. Agreed step: review pilot scope. Output uses those exact facts.

## Implemented
Structured fields, template generation and a verification checklist.

## Scope and limitations
No transcription or free-text extraction; dates are not invented and nothing is sent.
