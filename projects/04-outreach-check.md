# Outreach Check

**Supporting project · working offline prototype**

## Problem
A draft can contain an unfilled placeholder, competing asks or a promise the sender cannot support.

## Try it
Open [the demo](../index.html) and choose Outreach Check in the sidebar.

Paste a draft → inspect word count, question count and specific flags.

## Example
“Hi [name], guaranteed 100% results. Call? Send data?” flags the placeholder, promise and multiple asks.

## Implemented
Deterministic writing checks and explanations.

## Scope and limitations
No factual verification, deliverability scoring, personalization validation or universal quality score.
